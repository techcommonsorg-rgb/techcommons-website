Years 7–8

An adaptive pathway, not a fixed promise.

All students begin with a shared foundation in Python. As the class progresses, students who are ready will work with numerical data, tables and visualisation using libraries such as NumPy, pandas and Matplotlib.

Core Python

Syntax and variables; input and output; conditions; loops; lists; functions; structured problem-solving.

Applied Python

Working with data, numerical arrays, tables and charts using suitable libraries.

Adaptive extension

Pace and extension content depend on prior experience, progress during the course and the needs of the class.

Class scale

A maximum of approximately 25 students, with an operating plan designed for the larger group.

Before enrolment

Practical information.

Fees and class viability

Course fees are calculated to cover venue, equipment and operating costs. The final fee will be confirmed before enrolment opens. The amount per participant may decrease when more students enrol because operating costs are distributed across the class.

Minimum viable enrolment is not yet confirmed. If enrolment is too low to cover operating costs, TechCommons may postpone or cancel the class and will inform families promptly.

Refunds

Refunds will be determined according to the non-recoverable operating costs already committed for the course. Complete refund and cancellation terms will be provided before payment is requested.

Attendance

Course content builds progressively from week to week. A student who misses more than 40% of scheduled sessions may be unable to continue in the course.

Device and software

Students require access to a laptop capable of running a Python development environment. Suitable options include Visual Studio Code, PyCharm, or another local or browser-based environment suitable for the course. Final setup instructions will be provided before the first lesson.

Pre-course setup will be published later

The final page will include installation instructions, the selected Python version, editor options, a test script and troubleshooting guidance once the teaching environment is confirmed. No loan-device program is currently confirmed.

Supervision and adjustments

Sessions are taught by the TechCommons student instructor team and supervised by responsible adults throughout the program. Read the safeguarding approach. For adjustments, read the accessibility information and contact TechCommons before enrolment.
